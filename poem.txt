are we crammed - here - in heaven?
or are we larping home?
are we cosplaying the sedentary type?
can you hear our roots muttering?

"hey
measure your distance from catastrophe
and, also, you might have to forego desire"

i wonder:
did we fall from the hand of possibility?
every branch stared down into otherness
my future - i mean my culture - is composting
asking: how will *you* be reborn?
now that heartbreak is your shared art form
and longing has crow's feet and graying hair
